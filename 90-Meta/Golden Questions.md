# Golden Questions

Ten questions the vault should answer. Ask each in a fresh session (or via `bm_search`); record whether the right note appears in the **top 3**. Target: 8/10.

## Questions

1. What am I actively working on, and what is blocking each project?
2. Why did I choose the current stack for my second brain?
3. Who do I work with, and what does each person own?
4. What did I decide in the last 60 days that I might otherwise relitigate?
5. What are my standing preferences for how I like work delivered?
6. Which sources do I trust on your main domain?
7. What did I learn recently that changed how I work?
8. Which past decision has been superseded, and by what?
9. What do I keep re-explaining to people or AI?
10. What are the next three actions on my most active project?
